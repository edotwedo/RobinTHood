Security Deep Dive

Another Note on Security: What "giving access to my device" actually means.

When you enable local notes, Android pops up a system dialog asking for permission to access a folder. To most people, that sounds alarming—like handing an app the keys to your entire phone. Here is what is actually happening under the hood:

01 / STRICT SANDBOX

Locked to One Folder

RobinTHood uses Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF). You choose or create one specific folder (like Notes or Robin). Android cryptographically locks Robin inside that single folder. It is physically impossible for Robin to see your photos, texts, contacts, downloads, or other apps.

02 / PLAIN TEXT ONLY

No Executables or Binaries

Robin strictly reads and writes human-readable Markdown (.md) and text files under 1 MB. It refuses binaries, executables, and scripts. It cannot run code on your device, modify system files, or install background services.

03 / YOU OWN THE DATA

Zero Cloud Hostage

Big Tech AI stores your notes in their closed database so you can never cancel. Robin writes real .md files on your physical device. View, edit, or delete them anytime in your phone's Files app or Obsidian, and revoke folder permissions in Android settings in one tap.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

What does the 'T' stand for?

The "T" stands for "The" — as in Robin The Hood, the classic folk legend taking what was locked away behind high castle walls and returning it to the people. It also stands for Transparent, Truth, and Totally not the stock app.

How can this possibly be free?

RobinTHood routes queries through optimized open-source inference providers and edge infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers, OVHcloud, Kilo) that don't carry the massive overhead of Big Tech marketing. Basic non-intrusive banner ads cover server bandwidth.

How do I become a Google Play Beta tester?

Tap the Join the Google Play Beta button above. Sign in with the Google Account active on your Android phone, tap "Become a tester", and install the build directly from the Play Store.

Can I use it on Windows PC too?

Yes! We maintain an open-source Windows creative workspace release. Check our GitHub releases for the standalone Windows desktop EXE.
