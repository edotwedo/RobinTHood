RobinTHood for Android: Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026. Applies to the RobinTHood Android app (com.edotwedo.robinthood).

The short version

RobinTHood has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your chats are saved only on your phone. To answer you, the app sends your messages to the AI service you pick, and, when Web is on, sends a search to a search engine.

What stays on your phone

  • Your conversation history.
  • Your settings: chosen model, Web and music on/off, and any optional Ollama address or OpenRouter key you enter.

We never receive this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

What is sent, and to whom

  • AI services. When you send a message, the app sends your recent conversation and a short instruction to the model you chose, or to the next available free model if that one fails. These are OVHcloud AI Endpoints and the Kilo AI gateway, plus OpenRouter or your own Ollama server if you set one up. Their privacy policies cover what they do with requests.
  • Web search. When Web is on, a search query based on your message is sent to DuckDuckGo (or Wikipedia if DuckDuckGo fails). The app then opens the top results to read them, the same way a browser would.

These requests go straight from your phone to those services. They do not pass through any server of ours. Do not send passwords, card numbers or other information you would not want an AI service to see.

Reporting an answer

If you tap Report on a reply, the app sends that reply, the question before it, the reason you picked, any note you add, the app version and a random install ID to the RobinTHood developer's report service, so the report can be reviewed. Nothing is sent unless you report something.

Permissions

Internet access only, to reach the services above. The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, location or files.

Children

RobinTHood is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: use the developer contact email on the app's Google Play listing.
